Interested in plant physiology, microbiomes, or disease biology? Our research connects fundamental questions about plants and microbes with challenges in agriculture and conservation.

## Graduate research

To ask about potential MSc or PhD opportunities, email Connor with a short introduction, your research interests, and a CV. Tell us what interests you about our work and which questions you would like to explore.

## Postdoctoral research

If you see a connection between your expertise and our research, get in touch to discuss potential projects and fellowship opportunities. Include a CV and a brief outline of the ideas you would like to develop.

## Undergraduate research

Curious about research experience in the lab? Send a short introduction describing your interests, relevant coursework or experience, and the period when you would be available.

## Start a conversation

Email [Connor Fitzpatrick](mailto:connor.fitzpatrick@ucalgary.ca) with:

- Your current program, position, or background.
- The research topics that interest you and why.
- Your anticipated start date and availability.
- A CV, if you have one.

Opportunities depend on project fit, supervision capacity, and funding. An initial conversation helps establish what may be possible.
