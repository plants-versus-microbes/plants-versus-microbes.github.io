**Drought changes more than water availability. It also changes how plants acquire essential nutrients.**

## Overview

Plants can suppress iron uptake during drought. We study how this response is regulated and what it means for plant growth, crop performance, and nutritional quality.

## Key questions

- Why do plants reduce iron uptake under water limitation?
- Is this response conserved across plant lineages and crop species?
- Can drought responses be uncoupled from nutrient limitation?

## Approach

We combine controlled-environment experiments with plant genetics and physiology. Low-water agar systems and mutant screens help us separate the effects of water limitation from iron availability and identify the pathways that connect them.

## Why it matters

Understanding how plants regulate iron uptake during drought could help identify ways to maintain crop nutrition under increasingly dry conditions.
