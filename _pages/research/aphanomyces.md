**Understanding root disease requires seeing where infection develops and where hosts stop it.**

## Overview

Aphanomyces euteiches causes root rot in pulse crops, including pea and lentil. We study how infection progresses through roots and how resistant hosts restrict pathogen growth.

## Key questions

- How does infection progress through root tissues?
- Where and when is pathogen growth restricted in resistant hosts?
- Which host and pathogen genes are associated with these differences?

## Approach

We combine histology and laser capture microdissection to examine infection at cellular resolution. Comparisons between susceptible and resistant hosts help identify important infection stages and tissues for molecular analysis.

## Why it matters

This work provides a foundation for understanding resistance mechanisms and supporting the development of pulse crops with improved disease resistance.
