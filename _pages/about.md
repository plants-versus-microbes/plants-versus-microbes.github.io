      <p>We study how plants respond to environmental stress and how these responses shape interactions with microbial communities. Our work integrates plant physiology, genetics, and pathology to understand crop resilience.</p>
      <p>A central focus is how drought alters iron uptake and plant–microbe interactions, and how these processes influence crop performance, disease, and nutritional quality.</p>
