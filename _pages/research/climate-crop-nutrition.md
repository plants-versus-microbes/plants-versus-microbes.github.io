**Climate and soil conditions together shape the nutritional challenges facing crops.**

## Overview

Drought can coincide with soil conditions that limit iron availability. We investigate how these pressures interact and where they may place crop nutrition at risk as climates change.

## Key questions

- Where might crops face the greatest risk of iron deficiency?
- How do soil properties and drought interact to limit nutrient uptake?
- Which genetic or management approaches could help reduce these risks?

## Approach

We connect soil and climate datasets with greenhouse experiments, plant physiology, and genetics. Testing crops across soils and water treatments helps us understand the biological responses behind larger-scale patterns.

## Why it matters

By connecting plant biology with environmental data, we aim to improve predictions of nutritional stress and identify routes toward more resilient crops.
