**Disease risk depends on when spores and susceptible hosts meet.**

## Overview

Cronartium rust fungi threaten pine forests. We study how spore availability, host development, and environmental conditions combine to create periods of infection risk.

## Key questions

- When are rust spores present in the environment?
- How does host phenology influence infection risk?
- Can environmental monitoring help forecast periods of high disease hazard?

## Approach

We combine spore trapping, phenocam observations, and environmental monitoring across elevation gradients. These observations support models that connect spore abundance and host development with disease hazard.

## Why it matters

Forecasting infection risk can help inform forest monitoring, management, and conservation decisions.
