**Plant infection biology can reveal new targets for resistance to Verticillium stripe.**

## Overview

Verticillium longisporum is an emerging pathogen of Canadian canola. We investigate how it infects host plants and which pathogen traits contribute to disease.

## Key questions

- Which virulence factors contribute to infection?
- How does the pathogen spread through host tissues?
- Which host responses could guide resistance discovery in Brassica crops?

## Approach

We combine pathogen genomics, molecular genetics, and plant infection assays to investigate candidate effectors and host responses.

## Why it matters

Understanding the mechanisms behind disease can help identify resistance targets and inform future approaches to disease management.
